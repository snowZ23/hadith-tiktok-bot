Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Hadith TikTok Bot is a tool designed to generate Hadith images and upload them to an authorized TikTok account.

Information We Access

The application may access basic TikTok account information through TikTok Login Kit, including basic profile information required to identify the authorized account.

The application also requests permission to upload content to TikTok using the TikTok Content Posting API.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to authenticate the TikTok account and upload Hadith content requested by the authorized user.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Authentication

TikTok authentication is handled through TikTok's official authorization system. The application does not collect or store TikTok passwords.

Data Retention

Authentication information is retained only as necessary to provide the application's functionality. Users may revoke the application's access through their TikTok account settings.

Third-Party Services

This application uses TikTok services and APIs and is subject to TikTok's own privacy practices and policies.
